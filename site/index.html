Fauste Player

Radio playout for Linux, Windows and macOS. Independent players, sample-accurate overlapping mixes, a separate pre-listen output, and an audio engine the interface can never block.

Download Read the user guide

What it does

  • Independent players

    Four by default, any number configurable. Each has its own playlists, transport, countdown, meter and outputs.

  • Sample-accurate mixing

    In continuous mode the next track starts exactly at the MIX point and overlaps the fading end of the current one. Markers are found automatically and editable on the waveform.

  • CUE pre-listen

    Listen to any track on a separate device or channel pair before it goes on air, and load it as the next one.

  • Cartwall

    Pages of instant carts for jingles, effects and spots, with loops, exclusive carts and their own Main and Cue outputs.

  • Bit-perfect and DSD

    Exclusive access follows each file's sample rate; DSD tracks reach the converter unchanged as DoP or native DSD.

  • MIDI and remote control

    Learn players onto a MIDI controller, or operate everything over the network with an HTTP/JSON API, live events and OSC.

  • Built to survive

    A lost device reconnects by itself, saves are atomic with backups, and after a crash nothing goes on air on its own.

  • Eleven languages

    The interface follows the operating system language, or the one you choose in Settings. English and Spanish are hand-written; the other nine are AI-generated and may contain errors, so corrections from native speakers are welcome.
