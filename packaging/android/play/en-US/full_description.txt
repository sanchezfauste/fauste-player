Fauste Player puts music, jingles and spots on air. It is a radio playout application: several independent players run side by side, each with its own playlists, and tracks mix sample-accurately at their MIX points. The Android app runs the same playout core as the desktop application, with a layout made for touch.

PLAYERS AND PLAYLISTS
• One or more players, each with its playlists, in SINGLE or CONT mode
• Segues at each track's MIX point, cue-in, intro, outro and cue-out markers found by a background analysis, and markers you set with a long press on the waveform
• Pre-listen (CUE) on a separate output, such as headphones
• Loudness normalization
• Move tracks by dragging, or send one to another player right after its next track

CARTWALL
• Instant carts for jingles, effects and spots, on pages you swipe through
• Fold the cartwall on the Live screen to give the players more room

MADE FOR THE STUDIO
• Playout keeps going with the screen off or another app in front, with a notification while something plays
• Nothing goes on air by itself after a start, a restart or a crash
• A padlock guards against stray touches during a show: the transport and the carts keep working
• Choose the output of each player, the cartwall and their CUE: the speaker, wired headphones, a USB interface or a Bluetooth speaker
• Browse the music on your device by folder, artist or album, and search it

PRIVATE AND FREE
Fauste Player has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not ask for network access. It is free software under the GNU GPL (version 3 or later), and the same project runs on Linux, Windows and macOS.
